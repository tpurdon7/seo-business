# Discovery call guide

Use for a 30–45 minute first call. The aim is to establish fit and agree a sensible next step, not to provide a free strategy session.

## Before the call — 10 minutes

- Review website, Google Business Profile and obvious search presence.
- Record source, niche, location and known contact history.
- Note two specific observations and one open question.
- Confirm who is attending and their role.
- Open the pipeline record and call notes.

## Opening — 3 minutes

Suggested wording:

> Thanks for making the time. I’d like to understand what you want the business to achieve, what is happening with search now, and what has or has not worked before. I’ll then explain whether I think Better Search is a fit and the most sensible next step. We have about `[30/45]` minutes — does that work?

Ask permission before recording. Do not record by default.

## Discovery — 15–20 minutes

### Business and commercial context

- What does the business most want to grow over the next 6–12 months?
- Which services or treatments matter most commercially?
- Which locations or customer groups matter?
- What does a good enquiry look like?
- Roughly how valuable is a typical new client or patient? A range is fine.
- Are there capacity limits or services you do not want to promote?

### Current acquisition

- Where do enquiries come from now?
- What role does Google currently play?
- Do you know which pages or searches generate enquiries?
- Are you using an SEO agency, freelancer, developer or internal marketer?
- What have you tried, and what happened?

### Website and measurement

- Who controls the website and CMS?
- Is GA4 and Google Search Console set up?
- Are calls, forms or bookings tracked?
- Are there planned redesigns, migrations or service changes?

### Trust, compliance and approvals

- Who approves website content?
- Are there regulated claims, clinical review or brand requirements?
- What credible proof can be used: qualifications, reviews, memberships, process detail or outcomes that can be stated lawfully?
- How quickly can the team usually review work?

### Decision and timing

- Why is this being considered now?
- What would need to be true for this to feel worthwhile?
- Who else is involved in the decision?
- Is there an intended start date?
- Has a budget range been set?

## Reflect back — 5 minutes

Summarise:

- priority services and locations;
- current constraint;
- desired business outcome;
- measurement gaps;
- access/approval constraints;
- urgency and decision process.

Ask:

> Have I understood that correctly? Is there anything important I have missed?

## Qualification checklist

Score each item Yes / Unclear / No.

- The business offers a legitimate service Better Search understands.
- Search visibility could plausibly support the commercial goal.
- There is a specific priority, not only “more traffic”.
- The website can be accessed and changed.
- A decision-maker is involved or reachable.
- The client can provide accurate information and timely approvals.
- Expectations allow several months for ongoing organic work.
- Budget is compatible with the relevant package.
- The business is comfortable with evidence-based, non-guaranteed work.
- There is no ethical, legal or reputational concern.

### Disqualifiers or pause points

- Requests for guaranteed rankings, fabricated reviews or deceptive links.
- No practical ability to update the website.
- No owner for approvals or regulated claims.
- Serious unresolved website rebuild likely to invalidate immediate work.
- Expectations materially exceed budget or timeframe.
- Business, treatment or marketing practices appear unlawful or misleading.

## Recommend the next step — 5 minutes

### Visibility Sprint

Recommend when the problem is unclear, access/data is incomplete, or the prospect needs a prioritised plan before implementation.

### Growth Visibility

Recommend when priorities are reasonably clear and the prospect wants monthly implementation and tracking.

### Authority Growth

Recommend when competition is stronger and the work needs deeper content, trust and authority development.

Suggested wording:

> Based on what you’ve told me, I would recommend `[package]` because `[plain-English reason]`. The important boundary is `[boundary]`. I would not promise `[ranking/enquiry outcome]`; the work would give us a structured way to improve `[relevant inputs]` and measure what changes.

## Close

Agree one next action and date:

- send proposal by `[date]`;
- request missing access/information by `[date]`;
- book a second decision call;
- decline with a clear reason;
- place into follow-up for `[month/date]`.

Do not end with “I’ll send something over” without a date.

## After the call — 10 minutes

- Update pipeline stage and notes.
- Record package, value, objections, decision-makers and next action.
- Send a short recap within one working day.
- Create proposal only if scope, buyer, timing and next step are clear.
