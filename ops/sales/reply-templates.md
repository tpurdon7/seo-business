# Outreach reply templates

These are working drafts. Personalise the first line to the prospect's message and keep the reply in the existing thread.

## Interested

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks — happy to walk you through it.
>
> The three areas I'd start with are {{priority_1}}, {{priority_2}}, and {{priority_3}}. We can use a short call to decide whether you want to handle them internally or would like help implementing them.
>
> If helpful, you can choose a time here: {{booking_link}}
>
> Best,  
> Tom

If there is no approved booking link, offer two specific time windows instead. Do not use a placeholder in a live reply.

## Later

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Of course — thanks for letting me know.
>
> I’ll leave this with you and follow up in {{agreed_month_or_date}}. The audit link will remain here in the thread if you want to refer back to it.
>
> Best,  
> Tom

Set the CRM next action to the date the prospect requested. Do not continue the automated sequence.

## Already have an agency

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Understood, and I’m not looking to disrupt a relationship that’s working.
>
> Feel free to pass the audit to them if the observations are useful. If you ever want a second opinion on local or AI-search visibility, I’d be happy to look at a specific question without duplicating their work.
>
> Best,  
> Tom

Do not criticise the incumbent agency or push for a call.

## Price

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> It depends on whether you want a focused set of fixes or ongoing implementation.
>
> Before quoting, I’d want to confirm the pages involved, who controls the website, and whether you need recommendations only or hands-on changes. I can then give you a clear scope and fixed next step rather than a vague range.
>
> Would a short call be useful, or would you prefer I send those questions by email?
>
> Best,  
> Tom

Once package scope and approved prices are final, replace this with the relevant factual starting price and deliverables. Never invent or improvise discounts.

## Unsubscribe / not interested

> Understood — I won’t contact you again.
>
> Best,  
> Tom

Immediately set the record to `Do Not Contact`, record the date and reason, and stop all sequences. Do not add another pitch, question, or apology paragraph.
