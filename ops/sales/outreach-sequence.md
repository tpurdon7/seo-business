# Surrey clinics outreach sequence

Campaign: `surrey_clinics_2026_06_b01`

Status: prepared only. Do not send until the prospect's site, contact address, personalised observation, and public audit link have passed the pre-send QA checklist.

## Cadence

| Touch | Timing | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First touch | Day 0, Tuesday–Thursday | Specific observation and useful audit |
| Follow-up 1 | Day 3 | Make the value concrete with one priority fix |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 8 | Offer a practical three-point summary without requiring a call |
| Follow-up 3 | Day 14 | Close the loop and stop outreach |

Send during the clinic's local working day. Avoid Monday morning and Friday afternoon. Keep all follow-ups in the same thread.

## Required fields

- `{{contact_greeting}}`: first name only when publicly evidenced; otherwise `Hi,`
- `{{clinic_name}}`
- `{{town_or_area}}`
- `{{observed_issue}}`: one current, manually verified website observation
- `{{why_it_matters}}`: cautious explanation tied to clarity, trust, search understanding, or enquiry friction
- `{{priority_fix}}`: one specific recommendation grounded in the source evidence
- `{{audit_link}}`: the QA-approved public share URL with campaign UTMs; never use `PENDING_GENERATION`
- `{{sender_name}}`

## First touch — observation and audit

Subject options:

- `{{town_or_area}} page`
- `site structure`
- `contact clarity`
- `clinic pages`

Body:

> {{contact_greeting}}
>
> I was reviewing clinic websites around {{town_or_area}} and noticed something fixable on {{clinic_name}}'s site.
>
> {{observed_issue}}. {{why_it_matters}}.
>
> I put the finding and the fixes I'd prioritise into a short audit:
>
> {{audit_link}}
>
> Would it be useful to talk through the top three?
>
> Best,  
> {{sender_name}}

Keep this to roughly 70–110 words. Use one observation, not a catalogue of faults. Do not claim the clinic is losing rankings, traffic, patients, or revenue.

## Follow-up 1 — one practical priority

> Hi,
>
> One point worth pulling out from the audit: {{priority_fix}}.
>
> It would give prospective patients a clearer route to the relevant service and give search systems a more specific page to understand.
>
> If useful, I can send the three changes in priority order by email.
>
> Best,  
> {{sender_name}}

This follow-up must name a real recommendation from the prospect record. Do not introduce an unverified claim.

## Follow-up 2 — low-friction help

> Hi,
>
> I had another look at {{clinic_name}} and the opportunity still looks fairly contained rather than a full-site rebuild.
>
> I can send a concise three-point plan covering:
>
> 1. the first page or template to fix;
> 2. the local service page I'd prioritise;
> 3. the trust detail I'd move closer to the enquiry route.
>
> Want me to send that over?
>
> Best,  
> {{sender_name}}

Only use “rather than a full-site rebuild” when the audit supports that assessment. Otherwise replace the first paragraph with a factual summary of the observed opportunity.

## Follow-up 3 — close the loop

Subject: `close the loop`

> Hi,
>
> I'll close the loop after this.
>
> If improving {{clinic_name}}'s local search visibility becomes a priority later, the audit will still give you a useful starting point:
>
> {{audit_link}}
>
> No need to reply if the timing isn't right.
>
> Best,  
> {{sender_name}}

After this message, stop. Do not restart outreach unless the prospect replies, asks for a later follow-up, or there is a genuinely new and relevant reason to contact them.

## Personalisation guardrails

- Describe what was observed, not what is assumed.
- Use “the site states” for awards, review counts, years in business, registrations, or other first-party claims that have not been independently verified.
- Never claim a ranking position without a fresh, reproducible search check.
- Never imply access to analytics, conversions, revenue, patient numbers, or competitor data.
- Avoid overconfident causal language. Prefer “can make the route harder to scan” over “is costing you patients.”
- Do not use a named greeting unless the name and role are supported by the source pack.
- One email, one idea, one CTA.
