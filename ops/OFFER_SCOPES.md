## 1. Visibility Sprint — £1,000 one-time

### Best fit

A UK service business that needs a reliable diagnosis and prioritised plan before committing to ongoing work.

### Outcome

The client receives a clear view of current Google and AI-search visibility, the most important gaps, and a practical 90-day action plan.

### Included deliverables

- Kick-off questionnaire and one 45-minute call.
- Review of one primary website, up to 30 indexable URLs.
- Google visibility review using available search and analytics data.
- AI-search visibility spot-check across agreed commercial themes.
- Technical and on-page SEO review.
- Review of core service/treatment pages, local signals and trust signals.
- Competitor opportunity map covering up to three agreed competitors.
- Prioritised recommendations grouped into:
  - fix now;
  - improve next;
  - build later.
- One recommended page outline or homepage/service-page improvement example.
- 90-day action plan.
- Recorded or live 60-minute findings walkthrough.
- Final report supplied as a shareable document or workspace page.

### Boundaries

- Strategy and recommendations are included; implementation is not.
- No full backlink audit, digital PR campaign or link acquisition.
- No full content production.
- No website redesign, development or CMS migration.
- No paid advertising review unless separately agreed.
- No legal, medical, regulatory or accessibility certification.
- AI-search checks are observations at the time tested, not continuous monitoring.
- One consolidated round of factual corrections is included within five working days of delivery.

### Client dependencies

- Completed intake form.
- Website and business details.
- Read-only access to Google Search Console and GA4 where available.
- Google Business Profile access or exports where relevant.
- List of priority services, locations and up to three competitors.
- A named contact able to answer questions and approve factual details.

If access is unavailable, the sprint proceeds using public information and the report will state the resulting limitations.

### Timeline

- Start: after payment, signed scope and receipt of required inputs.
- Research and review: normally 7–10 working days.
- Findings call: normally within 10–15 working days of a complete handover.
- Delays in access or feedback move the delivery date.

### Completion point

The sprint is complete when the report, action plan and findings walkthrough have been delivered.

### Upgrade path

- Upgrade to Growth Visibility when the client wants Better Search to implement and manage the priority plan.
- Upgrade to Authority Growth when competitive positioning, deeper content and authority-building are also required.
- If a retainer starts within `[30]` days of the sprint, decide before sale whether any sprint fee is credited: `[no credit / £___ credit — confirm]`.

## 2. Growth Visibility — £1,100 per month

### Best fit

A service business with an established website that wants a focused monthly SEO and GEO programme, practical implementation and clear reporting.

### Outcome

Better Search works through an agreed priority backlog to improve the website’s clarity, technical foundations, search coverage and measurement over time.

### Included monthly service

- Initial baseline and 90-day strategy in month one.
- Monthly priority plan.
- Google ranking and search-performance tracking for an agreed keyword/theme set.
- AI-search visibility spot-checks for agreed commercial themes.
- Technical and on-page monitoring.
- Up to two substantive website work items per month, selected by expected value and effort. Examples:
  - improve one core service/treatment or location page;
  - prepare and implement metadata/internal-link improvements across a small page set;
  - resolve an agreed technical issue within ordinary CMS access;
  - create one detailed content brief;
  - improve FAQs, trust signals or answer-ready page sections.
- Content plan maintained for the next 60–90 days.
- One monthly report.
- One 45-minute monthly review call.
- Reasonable email questions relating to current work.

### What counts as a substantive work item

A contained piece of work that can normally be researched, prepared, checked and implemented within one working day. Large templates, development projects or broad rewrites must be split into phases or quoted separately.

### Boundaries

- Maximum of two substantive work items per month; unused capacity does not roll over.
- New long-form articles and full page copywriting are not included unless named as that month’s work item and sized accordingly.
- Developer-heavy fixes, custom code, redesigns and migrations are separately scoped.
- No guaranteed publishing cadence where the client controls approval or deployment.
- No backlink purchasing or guaranteed placements.
- Outreach, PR and paid media are not included.
- No medical, legal or regulatory sign-off; the client approves all specialist claims.
- One consolidated revision round per written deliverable.
- Meetings beyond the monthly call are separately agreed.

### Client dependencies

- CMS and relevant analytics access through secure invitations.
- Timely approval, normally within five working days.
- Accurate service, practitioner, location and compliance information.
- Access to a developer where the site cannot be changed through the CMS.
- A single decision-maker or consolidated feedback.

### Timeline and rhythm

- Month one: baseline, access check, measurement and first 90-day plan.
- Each month: agree priorities, complete work, quality-check, report and review.
- Meaningful organic-search movement commonly requires several months; timing depends on the starting point, competition, implementation and search-engine behaviour.

### Upgrade path

Move to Authority Growth when the priority backlog consistently requires more content depth, authority work, competitor analysis or a faster delivery pace.

## 3. Authority Growth — £1,600 per month

### Best fit

A high-trust service business in a more competitive market that needs deeper website, content and authority work alongside ongoing SEO and GEO management.

### Outcome

Better Search runs a broader growth programme aimed at making the business easier to discover, understand and trust across Google, relevant online sources and AI-assisted search.

### Included monthly service

Everything in Growth Visibility, with the following expanded scope:

- Up to three substantive website/content work items per month.
- Deeper quarterly competitor and opportunity review.
- Website and content architecture planning.
- Authority-building plan covering relevant profiles, citations, expert proof, partnerships and earned-link opportunities.
- Up to four authority opportunities researched or qualified per month.
- One outreach-ready authority asset, pitch angle or expert-contribution brief per month when prioritised.
- More detailed AI-search positioning review across agreed topics.
- Monthly report and one 60-minute strategy call.
- Quarterly 90-day roadmap refresh.

### Authority-building definition

Authority work means identifying and improving credible signals around the business. It can include profile consistency, source opportunities, expert-led content, original contributions and relevant relationship-based outreach. It does not mean buying links or promising coverage.

### Boundaries

- Maximum of three substantive work items per month; unused capacity does not roll over.
- Better Search cannot guarantee third-party publication, links, reviews, rankings or AI citations.
- Publication fees, sponsorships, specialist research, design, photography and third-party tools are not included unless approved in writing.
- High-volume PR outreach or full digital PR campaigns require separate scope.
- Custom development, redesign and migration are separately scoped.
- One consolidated revision round per written deliverable.
- The client must approve claims, credentials and regulated subject matter before publication.

### Client dependencies

All Growth Visibility dependencies, plus:

- Access to internal expertise for interviews or fact-checking.
- Approved practitioner/founder biographies, credentials and proof.
- Prompt approval for external pitches and public contributions.
- Willingness to participate in suitable authority opportunities.

### Timeline and rhythm

- Month one: baseline, authority inventory, priority fixes and 90-day roadmap.
- Monthly: implementation, content/authority work, monitoring and reporting.
- Quarterly: competitor review and roadmap reset.
- Authority work compounds over time and is normally evaluated over multiple months.

### Downgrade or exit path

- Move to Growth Visibility if the authority backlog or available client input reduces.
- On termination, Better Search supplies completed agreed deliverables and a current backlog/status summary, subject to payment and the signed agreement.

## Scope-change rule

When a request falls outside scope:

1. Confirm the desired outcome.
2. Explain which current commitment it would replace, if any.
3. Offer one of three routes:
   - swap it into the current month;
   - defer it to the backlog;
   - quote it as additional work.
4. Record the agreed route in writing before work starts.
