# Delivery playbook

Internal operating guide. The signed client scope overrides this playbook where they differ.

## Operating principles

- Work from commercial priorities, not vanity metrics.
- Preserve evidence: record the baseline and what changed.
- Make no ranking, traffic, AI-citation, enquiry or revenue guarantees.
- Never invent proof, credentials, reviews or outcomes.
- Client approves regulated, clinical, legal and material business claims.
- Keep scope visible; trade, defer or quote out-of-scope requests.
- Prefer secure delegated access to shared passwords.
- Do not publish AI-generated work without expert review.

## Standard workflow

1. Confirm scope, outcome and dependency.
2. Capture baseline and evidence.
3. Research and draft.
4. Perform factual, SEO and brand QA.
5. Obtain approval where required.
6. Implement or hand off.
7. Verify the live result.
8. Record work, impact hypothesis and next action.
9. Report plainly.

## Work item definition

Each work item must have:

- client/business priority;
- owner;
- included/out-of-scope status;
- source data/evidence;
- acceptance criteria;
- approval requirement;
- target date;
- status;
- live URL or deliverable link;
- QA record.

Statuses: Backlog → Ready → In progress → Client review → Ready to publish → Verified → Done / Blocked.

## Quality checklist

### Evidence and factual accuracy

- [ ] Names, locations, services and credentials match approved sources.
- [ ] No invented result, testimonial or statistic.
- [ ] Recommendations distinguish evidence from inference.
- [ ] Health, legal or financial claims are flagged for client review.
- [ ] Dates and time-sensitive facts are checked.

### Search quality

- [ ] Page intent is clear and tied to a real business priority.
- [ ] Title, H1, headings and body are consistent.
- [ ] Internal links are useful and use natural anchors.
- [ ] Canonical/indexing implications are checked.
- [ ] Local wording is genuine, not location swapping.
- [ ] Structured data is accurate and eligible before implementation.

### AI-search/GEO quality

- [ ] Key entities and services are unambiguous.
- [ ] Direct answers are concise and supported by the page.
- [ ] Claims can be traced to approved business evidence.
- [ ] Content is useful to people and not written only to manipulate AI tools.
- [ ] Any visibility check records date, prompts/themes and limitations.

### Conversion and trust

- [ ] Next action is obvious.
- [ ] Process, fit and practical details reduce uncertainty.
- [ ] Proof is visible only where permission exists.
- [ ] Copy avoids pressure, guarantees and unsupported superiority.

### Publishing

- [ ] Client approval is recorded where required.
- [ ] Mobile and desktop display checked.
- [ ] Links, forms and tracking checked.
- [ ] Live page is indexable only when intended.
- [ ] Before/after evidence saved.

## Visibility Sprint playbook

### Week 0 — Setup

- Confirm payment, scope and URL/competitor limits.
- Complete intake and access check.
- Agree primary services, locations and desired action.
- Record data limitations.

### Week 1 — Baseline and research

- Crawl/review up to 30 indexable URLs.
- Review Search Console/GA4 where available.
- Review core service, local and trust architecture.
- Check technical and on-page fundamentals.
- Run dated AI-search spot-checks.
- Review up to three competitors.

### Week 2 — Synthesis

- Prioritise issues by likely value, confidence and effort.
- Prepare competitor opportunity map.
- Prepare one page outline/improvement example.
- Build 90-day action plan.
- Complete internal QA.

### Delivery

- Send report at least one working day before or after walkthrough, according to agreed process.
- Walk through top findings, not every line.
- Confirm client-owned and Better Search-supported next steps.
- Allow one factual-correction round.
- Record completion and retainer fit without pressure.

### Required outputs

- Baseline/evidence record.
- Audit report.
- Competitor map.
- Page example/outline.
- Prioritised 90-day plan.
- Findings notes.

## Growth Visibility playbook

### Month 1

- Complete onboarding and access audit.
- Establish baseline and tracking limitations.
- Create 90-day strategy and prioritised backlog.
- Select up to two substantive work items.
- Deliver, approve, implement and verify.
- Report completed work and next priorities.

### Normal monthly cycle

#### Days 1–5

- Review previous actions and current data.
- Confirm up to two work items.
- Surface client dependencies immediately.

#### Days 5–20

- Research, draft and QA.
- Submit approval-sensitive work together where practical.
- Implement approved changes.

#### Days 20–month end

- Verify live work.
- Update metrics and evidence.
- Prepare report.
- Hold monthly call.
- Confirm next month’s likely priorities.

### Capacity rule

If a request will displace a committed item, tell the client what will move. Do not silently absorb work.

## Authority Growth playbook

Follow the Growth Visibility monthly cycle with up to three substantive work items, plus:

### Month 1

- Inventory current third-party profiles, citations, credentials, expert assets and earned coverage.
- Identify missing or inconsistent authority signals.
- Build authority opportunity backlog.

### Monthly

- Research/qualify up to four relevant authority opportunities.
- Prioritise one authority asset, contribution or outreach-ready angle where suitable.
- Track responses and placements honestly.
- Never buy, exchange or disguise links without explicit review and disclosure.

### Quarterly

- Re-review competitors and search landscape.
- Assess content/service architecture.
- Review authority work completed and pending.
- Refresh the 90-day roadmap.

## Client communication standards

- Acknowledge normal client messages within two working days.
- Flag blockers as soon as they threaten a date.
- Use one clear owner, action and due date.
- Do not bury scope changes inside general updates.
- Monthly calls focus on decisions, completed work and next priorities.

## Blocked-work rule

After five working days waiting for access/approval:

1. Send a concise reminder with the impact.
2. Offer an alternative unblocked task if one fits scope.
3. Move the delivery date where necessary.
4. Record the delay in the monthly report.

## End-of-engagement handover

- Confirm final paid deliverables.
- Supply current roadmap/backlog status.
- List live changes and pending approvals.
- Remove Better Search user access where appropriate.
- Archive credentials/access record.
- Request testimonial or case-study permission only after value has been delivered.
- Record renewal, referral or follow-up date without assuming consent.
